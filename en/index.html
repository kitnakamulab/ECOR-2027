01PURPOSE

Treating and regenerating organs outside the body A new stage in regenerative medicine

This inaugural meeting will be held to advance research in Extracorporeal Organ Regeneration (ECOR) and help translate its outcomes into clinical practice.

Bringing together knowledge and skills in medicine, pharmacy, science and engineering, we will explore organ perfusion, stem cells, regenerative medicine, gene therapy, organ measurement, testing and treatment technologies, mechanization, automation, DX and AI.

We plan to establish the vision of Extracorporeal Organ Regeneration and discuss research, academic development and international collaboration.

05SPONSORSHIP

To Companies and Organizations

We welcome support for ECOR2027 through corporate exhibits, advertisements on the official website and in the conference program booklet, luncheon seminars, donations, and other forms of sponsorship.

ECOR2027 is an international scientific conference dedicated to proposing and advancing Extracorporeal Organ Regeneration (ECOR) as an emerging field that aims to overcome current barriers in regenerative medicine and organ regeneration and open new possibilities for the future.

We warmly invite companies and organizations that share the aims of this meeting to support us through sponsorship, exhibition participation, advertising, or other contributions.
